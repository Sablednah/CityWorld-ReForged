# CityWorld: Biome Palettes

**Cities built to suit the biome they stand in.**

[CityWorld](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cityworld-reforged) builds every city from the same
materials wherever it stands. With Biome Palettes a desert city is sandstone, a taiga town is spruce and stone,
and a jungle one is mossy brick and bamboo.

| Theme | Where | Built of |
|---|---|---|
| Desert | deserts, dunes, oases, arid and dry land | sandstone, cut and smooth sandstone, pale terracotta |
| Badlands | badlands, mesas, canyons, outback | red sandstone and banded terracotta |
| Savanna | savannas, shrubland, scrub, steppe | acacia, mud brick, packed mud, orange terracotta |
| Tropical | jungles, rainforest, tropics, bamboo | mossy stone brick, jungle wood and bamboo |
| Swamp | swamps, bayous, marshes, bogs, mangroves | mangrove and dark oak, mud brick, mossy cobble |
| Boreal | taiga, pine, spruce, fir and redwood forest, snowy woods | spruce, stone brick, cobble, deepslate |
| Frozen | snowy plains, tundra, ice, glaciers | spruce, calcite, diorite, white and pale blue, packed ice |
| Coastal | beaches, shores, coves | whitewash, birch, sandstone, prismarine |
| Mountain | peaks, cliffs, highlands, windswept hills, volcanoes | stone brick, andesite, deepslate, spruce |
| Blossom | cherry and sakura groves, wisteria, jacaranda | cherry wood, pink and white terracotta, calcite |

Plains, forests, birch woods and meadows keep CityWorld's own look.

- **One building, one look.** Where a biome ends in the middle of a city, the buildings either side differ; no
  building is half of each.
- **Works with biome mods.** Biomes are matched by name and by tag, so a mod's deserts, taigas and swamps join
  the right theme without being listed: Biomes O' Plenty, Terralith, Regions Unexplored, Dreamwoods and others.
- **Works on vanilla land.** With CityWorld's cities in a vanilla world, each city takes the theme of the biome
  at its centre, as a village does.
- **Works with Neutral Palette.** Install both for themed cities with no valuable blocks in the walls.

The shape of a city does not change: the same seed gives the same streets and buildings, in different materials.
Windows, streets, interiors and schematic buildings are left alone.

## Install

1. **CityWorld 7.3.0 or later.**
2. Drop the Biome Palettes jar in `mods` beside it.

One jar works on every Minecraft version CityWorld ships for: 1.20.1 (Forge) and 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.2, 26.3
(NeoForge). It changes how buildings are made, so it shows in **newly generated chunks**: start a new world, or
head for land you haven't explored.

## Change it

It's a plain datapack inside. Which biomes a theme covers is one small file per theme, and what a theme builds
with is a block tag per building part, so a pack author can retune a theme, add a mod's blocks to one, or make a
new theme. The format is on CityWorld's wiki under
[Palettes by biome](https://github.com/Sablednah/CityWorld-ReForged/wiki/Building-Palettes#palettes-by-biome-from-73).
